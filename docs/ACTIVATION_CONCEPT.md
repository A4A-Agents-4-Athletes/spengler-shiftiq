# Spengler ShiftIQ: Multi-Channel Activation Concept
**ThinkSport Innovation Challenge: Data-hack 2026**
**Deliverable:** Lightweight Activation Concept Document
**Partners:** Spengler Cup Davos, scoreTec, SRF Sport, ThinkSport

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The primary challenge identified by Spengler Cup organisers is that digital visitors **"check the score and leave."** This document outlines a cohesive activation concept showing how **Spengler ShiftIQ** integrates into four existing touchpoints across the tournament ecosystem to convert fleeting attention into sustained fan discovery and partner value.

---

## 2. Touchpoint Architecture

### A. The Official Match Center (`spenglercup.ch`)
- **Placement:** Positioned immediately beneath the live scoreboard on every game page (replacing or augmenting the traditional static text box score).
- **Core Value:**
  - Visitors don't just see "4 - 2 Final"; they see a visual scrubber of the 3 most decisive shifts of each period.
  - Interactive "Player DNA" cards let fans compare their favorite players head-to-head.
- **Projected Impact:** Triples average time-on-page (from ~45 seconds to 2.5+ minutes) and provides premium branded digital inventory for title sponsors (e.g. UBS, Würth).

---

### B. SRF Sport TV Broadcast Integration
- **Context & Audience Scale (from Official 2025 Event Report):**
  - Over **2.7 million TV viewers** watched Spengler Cup 2025 live across SRG SSR networks (SRF zwei, RTS, RSI) over 81 broadcast hours.
  - The **HC Davos vs. U.S. Collegiate Selects Final on 31 December reached a staggering 54.6% market share on SRF zwei**, with ratings up **+46% in the key 15–59 demographic**.
  - Total tournament media presence generated **104 million contacts** across 2,501 broadcasts and articles.
- **Live Overlays & Second-Screen QR Code:**
  - Capitalizing on the 54.6% live broadcast share, automated Wisehockey lower-third graphics trigger during play stoppages: *"Stransky Powerplay Goal: 100.1 km/h shot · 0.122 xG"*.
  - A discreet on-screen QR code allows the 2.7M viewers to instantly open ShiftIQ on mobile without downloading an app: *"Scan to explore the 2D shift replay, skater vectors, and tactical breakdown."*
- **Commentator Enablement:**
  - Studio experts (e.g. Jann Billeter) receive a tablet controller to pause, scrub, and annotate skater vector trails during intermission tactical breakdowns.

---

### C. In-Stadium Experience (Eisstadion Davos)
- **Context:** Eisstadion Davos holds 6,547 passionate spectators. Intermissions are 18 minutes long.
- **Center-Ice Jumbotron Trivia:**
  - During the 1st and 2nd intermissions, the giant video cube runs the **"Ice IQ Fan Vote"**:
    *"Who skated the longest distance in Period 1?"*
  - Fans connect via the arena Wi-Fi portal or scan an in-seat QR code to vote.
  - The live jumbotron screen updates real-time percentages before revealing the Wisehockey telemetry answer and highlighting the winning fan section.

---

### D. Social Media & Digital Clips (TikTok, Instagram Reels, X)
- **Automated Video Snippets:**
  - The Python pipeline automatically extracts 10-second MP4 video exports combining real broadcast video with the synchronized 2D vector trail and speed badge.
  - Formatted vertically (9:16) for TikTok and Instagram Reels with snappy hooks:
    *"How Andres Ambühl created a Spengler Cup goal without touching the puck."*
  - Direct swipe-up / bio link driving fans back to the full interactive match center.
