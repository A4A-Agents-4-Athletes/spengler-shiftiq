### Scene-by-Scene Script

#### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Visual:** Fast-paced footage of Eisstadion Davos at night, roaring crowd, then a screen recording of a traditional ice hockey box score table full of numbers.
- **Voiceover:**
  *"Ice hockey is the fastest team sport on the planet. It’s played at 40 kilometers an hour, decided in split seconds, and packed with raw adrenaline. Yet when fans check the match results online, what do they see? A static spreadsheet of numbers. They check the score—and leave. Great performances deserve more than a number. Welcome to Spengler ShiftIQ."*

#### Scene 2: The Decisive Shift & Dual Lens (0:30 – 1:15)
- **Visual:** Screen capture of the Spengler ShiftIQ application. Host selects "Matej Stransky Breakout Goal" and scrubs the interactive timeline. We see the puck vector and skater trails animating smoothly across the ice.
- **Voiceover:**
  *"ShiftIQ brings Wisehockey data to life. Instead of reading that Stransky scored at 8:42 of the second period, fans can scrub through the decisive shift on an interactive 2D rink. Look at Andres Ambühl at the red line: our system highlights his subtle defensive stick-check that forced the turnover and sparked the 37.9 km/h counter-attack in 4.2 seconds.
  With our Dual-Lens switcher, casual fans get vivid human storytelling, while coaches and analysts toggle to Pro Mode to see exact spatial coordinates, acceleration bursts, and expected goal spikes."*

#### Scene 3: Player DNA & Tactical Lineup Builder (1:15 – 1:50)
- **Visual:** Transition to the "Player DNA & Clash" tab. Ambühl vs. Nazar radar chart appears. Host switches to the "Tactical Line Builder" and selects a Power Play unit.
- **Voiceover:**
  *"Beyond the replay, fans can explore Player DNA: hexagonal radar profiles and tactical archetypes like 'The Slot Disruptor' and 'The Blue-Line Wall'.
  Want to see what happens if you pair Davos' veteran grinders with Team USA's collegiate speedsters? The Tactical Line Builder calculates real-time chemistry ratings and simulates a 60-second shift with instant feedback on strengths and vulnerabilities."*

#### Scene 4: Broadcast & Arena Activation (1:50 – 2:20)
- **Visual:** Transition to "Broadcast & Arena Hub". Showing the SRF TV lower-third overlay with QR code, followed by the Eisstadion Davos jumbotron intermission fan vote.
- **Voiceover:**
  *"ShiftIQ is built for the entire Spengler Cup ecosystem. During live broadcasts on SRF, commentators can surface instant shift breakdowns, while fans scan a TV QR code to explore the play on their mobile second-screen. During 18-minute intermissions, the Ice IQ challenge turns downtime into active fun for thousands of fans inside Eisstadion Davos."*

#### Scene 5: Technical Feasibility & Close (2:20 – 3:00)
- **Visual:** High-resolution responsive views across iPhone, iPad, and desktop. Brief glimpse of the automated Python data pipeline processing raw CSVs. Closing logo card with Spengler Cup and ThinkSport branding.
- **Voiceover:**
  *"Engineered with a lightweight, high-performance React and SVG architecture, ShiftIQ is fully responsive, mobile-first, and ready to ingest Wisehockey match data seamlessly.
  By bridging the gap between numbers and emotions, Spengler ShiftIQ turns spectators into explorers. Thank you—and see you at the Spengler Cup in Davos!"*
