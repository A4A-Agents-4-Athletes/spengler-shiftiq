Watch Webinar #2: “Making Data Playable”, held on 6 October 2026 as part of the Spengler Cup Data-hack Sport Innovation Challenge.


Powered by ThinkSport, in partnership with scoreTec, and in collaboration with the Spengler Cup, the challenge asks university students and independent tech-builder teams:


How can we turn real ice hockey performance data into an experience people actually want to explore?


In this webinar, Michael Vuilleumier and Matteo Bonzon take participants beyond simply presenting sports data and explores how interaction design and gamification can make ice hockey data more intuitive, engaging and playable.


The session encourages teams to think about the experience from the user's perspective: What makes someone want to click, play, guess, compare or explore, rather than simply scroll through statistics? And how can interaction help both hockey experts and complete newcomers better understand why a player's performance matters?


The session helps participants explore:


How to turn sports data into an interactive experience
* How interaction design can make complex information more intuitive and accessible
* How game mechanics and playful interactions can encourage users to explore data
* How to design for both hockey newcomers and experienced fans
* How to move from simply displaying statistics to creating an experience that encourages curiosity, learning and engagement
* How these principles can be applied to their Data-hack prototypes
* The challenge concludes with a Final Pitching Day on 7 December 2026 at The Circle in Zurich.
