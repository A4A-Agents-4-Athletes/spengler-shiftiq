Oct 12, 2026  #SICDataHack #SportInnovation #IceHockey
Watch Webinar #3: “How SRF Builds Around Sport Data”, held on 9 October 2026 as the final webinar of the Spengler Cup Data-hack Sport Innovation Challenge series.


Powered by ThinkSport, in partnership with scoreTec and in collaboration with the Spengler Cup, the challenge asks university students and independent tech-builder teams:


How can we turn real ice hockey performance data into an experience people actually want to explore?


In this webinar, participants discover how television and media professionals use sport data to develop compelling stories, interactive content and engaging second-screen experiences for fans.


The session encourages teams to think beyond simply displaying statistics: How can data enrich a live sports broadcast? Which insights are meaningful to viewers? And how can digital experiences complement the action without distracting from it?


The session helps participants explore:


How SRF builds content and stories around sport data
* How data can enhance live broadcasts and second-screen platforms
* How to select insights that are relevant and understandable for fans
* How interactive content can complement the viewing experience
* How to balance information, entertainment and simplicity
* How these media principles can be applied to Data-hack prototypes
* The challenge deliverables, next steps and final-event logistics
* 

This webinar concludes the three-part expert series supporting participants throughout the development of their projects.


The challenge culminates in a Final Pitching Day on 7 December 2026 at The Circle in Zurich.
