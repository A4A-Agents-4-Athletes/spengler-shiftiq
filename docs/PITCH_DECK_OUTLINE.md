# Spengler ShiftIQ: 10-Slide Final Pitch Deck Outline
**ThinkSport Innovation Challenge: Data-hack 2026**
**Venue:** The Circle Convention Center, Zurich Airport · 7 December 2026
**Presenter:** Kamilla Gafurzianova
**Judging Panel:** Hockey Club Davos (HCD), Spengler Cup Organisers, Swiss Ice Hockey Federation (SIHF)

---

### Slide 1: Title & The Core Paradox
- **Headline:** Spengler ShiftIQ: Turning Ice Hockey Data Into Active Discovery
- **Sub-headline:** Great performances deserve more than a number.
- **The Paradox:** Ice hockey is the fastest team sport in the world, packed with raw emotion and split-second genius. Yet its digital statistics look like spreadsheets. Visitors check the final score (e.g. Davos 4 - 2 USA) and immediately leave.
- **Our Mission:** Transform passive visitors into active participants through visual shift storytelling.

---

### Slide 2: The Two Audiences Dilemma
- **Audience A: The Newcomer & Casual Supporter**
  - Wants to feel the drama, understand why everyone stood up, and learn what happened without feeling overwhelmed by technical jargon.
- **Audience B: The Hardcore Fan, Coach & TV Analyst**
  - Wants spatial coordinates, micro-gap measurements, transition velocities, and xG spikes.
- **The Solution:** A **Dual-Lens Architecture** that toggles seamlessly between **"Fan Story Lens"** (accessible narrative) and **"Pro Analyst Lens"** (deep telemetry).

---

### Slide 3: Flagship Feature: The Decisive Shift (Interactive 2D Rink)
- **Concept:** Reconstructing game-defining plays through time-synchronized Wisehockey tracking.
- **Demonstration:**
  - Matej Stransky's 37.9 km/h breakaway goal.
  - Showing Andres Ambühl's neutral zone stick-lift that occurred 4 seconds earlier.
  - Timeline scrubber, player velocity trails, and spatial pressure cones.
- **The Story:** *"Goals don't start at the red line; they start with the invisible work along the boards."*

---

### Slide 4: Feature 2: Player DNA & Tactical Archetypes
- **Beyond Spreadsheet Tables:**
  - Classifying players into intuitive role archetypes: *The Slot Disruptor* (Ambühl), *The High-Speed Finisher* (Stransky), *The Playmaking Maestro* (Corvi), *The Blue-Line Wall* (Fora).
  - 6-pillar hexagonal radar charts: Speed, Puck IQ, Shooting, Physicality, Defense, Transition.
  - Interactive head-to-head comparison cards highlighting tactical matchups.

---

### Slide 5: Feature 3: Tactical Dream Line Builder
- **Gamified Participation:**
  - Fans select 3 forwards and 2 defensemen for high-leverage situations (Power Play, Penalty Kill, 3v3 Overtime).
  - Real-time **Tactical Chemistry Rating (0-100)** calculated from player telemetry synergies.
  - Immediate feedback on unit strengths (e.g. "Elite one-timer threat") and vulnerabilities ("Risk of counter-attacks").
  - 60-second shift simulation button.

---

### Slide 6: Feature 4: Ice IQ (Intermission Challenge)
- **Second-Screen Engagement:**
  - Bite-sized trivia challenges testing fan memory and data intuition during 18-minute intermissions.
  - Examples: *"Who skated over 5.5 kilometers tonight?"*, *"Who blasted the 157.6 km/h one-timer?"*
  - Instant reward loop with telemetry proof from Wisehockey event logs.

---

### Slide 7: Activation Blueprint (Ecosystem Integration)
- **1. SRF Sport TV Broadcast:** Lower-third telemetry badges with QR codes inviting viewers to scrub the play on their phones.
- **2. Eisstadion Davos Jumbotron:** Live intermission fan votes for "Hardest Working Skater" using in-stadium Wi-Fi.
- **3. Spengler Cup Match Center (`spenglercup.ch`):** Embeddable widget turning static box scores into an interactive sandbox.

---

### Slide 8: Technical Architecture & Feasibility
- **Frontend:** Lightweight, blazing-fast React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS application (sub-second load time, zero external heavy dependencies).
- **Visualization:** Clean SVG 2D rink engine with infinite mobile pinch-to-zoom and 60fps smooth playback.
- **Data Engine:** Automated Python ingestion pipeline transforming Wisehockey CSV exports into compressed JSON bundles.
- **Design Standard:** Architectural modern corner radii, zero-orphan multilingual typography, and high-definition mobile-first rendering.

---

### Slide 9: Business & Fan Engagement Impact (Backed by 2025 Data)
- **Massive TV Reach to Convert:** The 2025 HC Davos vs. U.S. Collegiate Selects final commanded an extraordinary **54.6% market share on SRF zwei** (over 2.7M live TV viewers across Switzerland, +46% growth in the 15–59 age group). ShiftIQ transforms this passive broadcast audience into active second-screen users.
- **Time on Site:** Projected 3.5x increase in Spengler Cup match center dwell time (from ~45s bounce to 2.5+ minutes active exploration).
- **Sponsor & Partner Value:** High-visibility digital inventory for Spengler Cup partners (e.g., UBS, Würth, scoreTec) integrated into the 2D replay scrubber and intermission quiz cards.
- **Social Media Synergy:** Captures moments for the tournament's 30 million social media contacts (14.8M Instagram video views) via automated vertical telemetry highlights.
- **Global & Multilingual Reach:** Bilingual interface (German / English) tailored for Swiss, North American, and international hockey fans.

- **Immediate Next Step:** Showcase live pilot at Spengler Cup 2026 in Davos.
- **Long-Term Potential:** Scalable across the entire Swiss National League (NL) and international IIHF tournaments.
